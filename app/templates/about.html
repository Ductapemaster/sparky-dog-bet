Sparky

Meet Sparky! He's a 6 year old mixed breed rescue pup that loves toys, treats, and adventuring. When he's not busy contributing to scientific research through his job, he's an active guy that likes playing frisbee and soccer. Initially surrendered to Oakland Animal Services with his puppies, Sparky was adopted during a large dog adoption event three years ago and now lives happily in Alameda with his parents, Dan and Zoe.

Sparky is an excellent road trip companion and has become quite a well-traveled pup, joining us on all sorts of adventures through 6 states (so far!). He went camping for the first time last summer and absolutely loved it, especially the time spent at the river.

His favorite hobbies include chewing up the biggest sticks he can find, sticking his head a bit too far out of the car window, playing backyard fetch, and settling down for some cozy couch snuggles. He's a big guy, weighing in over 100 lbs, but that's just because he has a lot of love to give. He is loved by friends, family, and even the neighbors — and is an all around good boy!

We've always wondered what mix of breeds could produce such a perfectly chaotic dog, so we finally sent off a DNA test. The results are sealed until the reveal — that's where you come in. The guest whose breed guess is closest to Sparky's actual DNA breakdown wins the pot. Good luck, and may the best amateur canine geneticist win.
